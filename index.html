Product Description

What It Is
Dragonblood Anti-Ageing Serum is a lightweight botanical serum built around dragon blood extract - the deep-red resin of the dragon tree - combined with ceramide NP and glossy ganoderma. It’s designed to repair the skin barrier, lock in moisture and smooth the look of fine lines. One bottle: 30 ml.

How to Use
Morning and night, apply 3–4 drops to clean, dry skin. Gently press into the face and neck until absorbed, then follow with your moisturiser and (in the morning) SPF.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does it do?
It supports your skin’s natural barrier, hydrates deeply and helps restore an even, healthy glow as part of a daily routine.

Is it suitable for daily use?
Yes. The formula is lightweight and gentle, designed for use morning and night.

Which skin types can use it?
Most skin types, including dry, combination and sensitive skin. Patch-test first if your skin is very reactive.
