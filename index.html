What It Is

Dragonblood Anti-Ageing Serum is a lightweight botanical serum built around dragon blood extract — the deep-red resin of the dragon tree — combined with ceramide NP and glossy ganoderma. It’s designed to repair the skin barrier, lock in moisture and smooth the look of fine lines. One bottle: 30 ml.

How to Use

Morning and night, apply 3–4 drops to clean, dry skin. Gently press into the face and neck until absorbed, then follow with your moisturiser and (in the morning) SPF.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dragonblood serum?

A daily anti-ageing serum built around dragon blood extract, ceramide NP and glossy ganoderma, designed to repair the skin barrier, hydrate deeply and restore a healthy glow.

Is it suitable for daily use?

Yes. The formula is lightweight and gentle, and it’s designed to be used morning and night as part of a simple daily routine.

Which skin types can use it?

It suits most skin types, including dry, combination and sensitive skin. If you have very reactive skin, patch-test on your inner arm first.

How long until I see results?

Many customers notice softer, more hydrated skin within days. For tone and texture changes, give it 3–4 weeks of consistent use.

What if it doesn’t work for me?

You’re covered by our 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee. If you’re not happy, contact us for a full refund — no questions asked.

Can I use it with other skincare?

Yes — apply it after cleansing and before moisturiser. If you use strong actives (retinol, exfoliating acids), introduce it gradually.
